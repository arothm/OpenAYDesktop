The most a chat sends with each request. Past it, older messages are replaced by a summary the model writes (your own messages stay, shortened), kept until the next one. Full (recommended) summarizes only when the model's window is nearly full: Ollama bills the unchanged start of a conversation at its much cheaper cached-input rate, so resending history costs little. Economy and Balanced send fewer tokens per request and suit models without cached-input pricing or very long chats.

Token limits count tokens sent and generated, as Ollama reports them; 0 turns a limit off. When a limit is reached, replies stop with a message until you raise it. Subtasks count toward every limit. A step is one request to the model (a round of tool calls, or the answer); a reply that reaches the step limit pauses with a Continue button. When your Ollama balance is at or below the pause amount, running replies summarize their progress and pause, then continue by themselves once you recharge.
